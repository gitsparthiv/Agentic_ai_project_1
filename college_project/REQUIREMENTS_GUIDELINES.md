## 🎯 Formal Problem Statement

### Academic Problem Statement (For College Synopsis, Title Registration & Report Introduction)
> Modern enterprises face an unprecedented volume of user feedback, product reviews, and public discourse across digital channels. Within this unstructured stream lie critical consumer grievances, allegations of product failure, regulatory non-compliance, and defamatory attacks that pose severe corporate legal liabilities.
> 
> Currently, organizations rely on manual legal monitoring or simplistic sentiment analysis tools that lack legal domain understanding. This creates a critical operational gap: legal departments only become aware of high-risk allegations after public escalation, regulatory notices, or lawsuits have already been initiated. Furthermore, cross-referencing global complaints against multi-jurisdictional statutory frameworks (such as Consumer Protection Acts, IT/Cyber Regulations, Defamation statutes, and Data Privacy laws) remains labor-intensive, time-lagged, and economically prohibitive.
> 
> There is an acute need for an autonomous, multi-agent artificial intelligence framework capable of:
> 1. Ingesting continuous public discourse referencing enterprise products/features;
> 2. Classifying sentiment, verifying grievance validity, and filtering out non-actionable noise; and
> 3. Autonomous multi-jurisdictional statutory consultation—cross-referencing actionable claims against statutory law books, citing exact violation sections, and generating comprehensive legal risk dossiers for executive action.

## 🎯 College Prescribed Steps & Evaluation Criteria

### Step 1: Topic Selection & Problem Definition
- **Objective:** Finalize project domain, title, and problem statement approved by college guide.
- **Status:** ✅ **Selected: LegalAdvisor AI**
- **Deliverables:** Problem statement, abstract, and scope document.

### Step 2: Literature Survey / Market Research
- **Existing Solutions Analysis:** Traditional Social Listening tools (Brandwatch, Hootsuite) vs. LegalTech databases (Westlaw, Manupatra).
- **Research Gaps:** Lack of automated pipeline bridging raw public sentiment to statutory legal clause extraction.
- **Proposed Value Proposition:** Real-time multi-agent autonomous triage + RAG statutory legal advisor.

### Step 3: Requirement Specifications (SRS)
- **Functional Requirements:**
  - Automated post scraper/ingestor (Agent 1).
  - Sentiment & validity classifier (Agent 2).
  - Statutory retrieval engine & legal report generator (Agent 3).
- **Non-Functional Requirements:** Low latency, citation fidelity, hallucination control.

### Step 4: System Design & Feasibility Study
- **Agentic AI Architecture & Tooling Strategy:** LangGraph multi-agent state graph, Gemini LLM, Vector DB.

### Step 5: Implementation & Milestone Tracking
- **Sprint 1:** Data Ingestion & Normalization pipeline.
- **Sprint 2:** Sentiment & Validity Triage filter.
- **Sprint 3:** Multi-Jurisdiction Statutory RAG & section mapping.
- **Sprint 4:** Executive Dossier export & Client dashboard.
