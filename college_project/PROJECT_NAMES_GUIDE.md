## 1. Executive Summary & Selection Criteria

For a final-year engineering/business capstone, the project name needs to balance:
1. **Academic Rigor:** Sounds credible to university evaluation panels, professors, and external examiners.
2. **Technical Accuracy:** Reflects the 3-agent pipeline (Ingestion ➔ Sentiment & Validity Triage ➔ Multi-Jurisdictional Statutory Consultation).
3. **Enterprise Value:** Conveys high-impact B2B corporate legal utility.

---

## 2. Category 1: Academic & Research-Grade Titles
*Best suited for research publications, thesis cover pages, and formal college portals.*

### 1. **LexSentinel**
- **Etymology:** *Lex* (Latin for Law) + *Sentinel* (A guard whose job is to stand and keep watch).
- **Sub-Title:** *Autonomous Multi-Agent Legal Intelligence and Corporate Risk Assessment System*
- **Why it fits:** Highlights the vigilant, 24/7 autonomous monitoring of public discourse and proactive statutory legal defense.

### 2. **JurisIntellect AI**
- **Etymology:** *Juris* (Law / Legal Right) + *Intellect* (Cognitive capacity & reasoning).
- **Sub-Title:** *An Autonomous Multi-Agent Framework for Cross-Jurisdictional Legal Compliance & Public Sentiment Triage*
- **Why it fits:** Emphasizes multi-jurisdictional legal reasoning rather than simple keyword filtering.

### 3. **LexiGuard**
- **Etymology:** *Lexi* (Legal lexicon / words) + *Guard* (Protection).
- **Sub-Title:** *Multi-Agent Sentiment Triage and Statutory Violation Identification Platform*
- **Why it fits:** Explicitly bridges the gap between language (user comments/reviews) and legal shielding.

### 4. **SentiLaw AI**
- **Etymology:** *Sentiment* (Emotion/Feedback) + *Law* (Statutory rules).
- **Sub-Title:** *Automated Brand Sentiment Analysis & Multi-Jurisdictional Statutory Risk Consultation*
- **Why it fits:** Directly maps Agent 2's role (sentiment) to Agent 3's role (law).

## 3. Category 2: Enterprise SaaS & Modern Tech Names
*Best suited if pitching the project as an enterprise product or startup MVP.*

### 1. **VeriLex**
- **Etymology:** *Veritas* (Latin for Truth / Validity) + *Lex* (Law).
- **Tagline:** *Turning Public Discourse into Verified Legal Intelligence.*
- **Core Concept:** Emphasizes Agent 2's validity check (separating false rumors/spam from genuine claims) before initiating legal audit.

### 2. **LexAudit AI**
- **Tagline:** *Autonomous Multi-Jurisdictional Brand & Legal Risk Auditing.*
- **Core Concept:** Presents the software as an automated audit companion for corporate legal counsels.

### 3. **JurisPulse**
- **Tagline:** *Real-Time Public Feedback & Legal Exposure Intelligence.*
- **Core Concept:** Captures the real-time "pulse" of social platforms combined with instant statutory risk analysis.

### 4. **ClauseWatch**
- **Tagline:** *Autonomous Statutory Compliance & Defamation Sentinel.*
- **Core Concept:** Focuses on pinpointing exact legal clauses and statutory sections violated.

## 4. Category 3: Meaningful Engineering Acronyms
*Classic capstone presentation style; college viva panels appreciate structured acronyms.*

### 1. **A.E.G.I.S.**
- **Full Form:** **A**utonomous **E**valuation of **G**rievances & **I**ntelligent **S**tatutory compliance
- **Significance:** In mythology, the *Aegis* represents divine protection and a shield—symbolizing corporate legal protection.

### 2. **J.U.R.I.S.**
- **Full Form:** **J**urisdictional **U**ser-feedback **R**eview & **I**ntelligence **S**ystem
- **Significance:** Professional, grounded, and unmistakably focused on legal governance.

### 3. **L.E.X.I.S.**
- **Full Form:** **L**egal **E**xposure e**X**traction & **I**ntelligence **S**entinel
- **Significance:** Highlights automated extraction of statutory risk exposure.
